# Teaching
I have taught abstract algebra, real and complex analysis,
as well as cryptography and data visualization with Python at
the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science of the University
of Bucharest, and at the Faculty of Applied Sciences at the “Politehnica”
National University of Science and Technology in Bucharest.

All of my courses and seminars have written notes and problem sets,
but they are only available in Romanian.

You can find them in the [Romanian section of this website]({{< relref path="/didactica/_index.md" lang="ro" >}}).

Currently, I am working to expand my reach through [Poligon Educational](https://poligon-edu.ro/en),
where I will also publish lecture notes and exercise sets.
