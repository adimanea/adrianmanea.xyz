# Research

I have a PhD in mathematics (homological algebra) since 2016,
with a thesis coordinated by [Professor Dragoș Ștefan](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=R0lOY0gAAAAJ&hl=ro)
from the University of Bucharest.

I have published three articles in peer-reviewed journals:

* *On Koszulity of Finite Graded Posets* (coauthored with Dragoș Ștefan), J. Algebra Appl. 16, 1750139 (2017), 20 pages (arXiv [here](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1605.05458)).
* *The Ext Ring of a Koszul Ring*, Bull. Math. Soc. Sci. Math. Roumanie, Tome 59 (107), No. 1, 2016, pp. 51-63 (full article [here](https://ssmr.ro/bulletin/pdf/59-1/articol_5.pdf)).
* *Further Properties and Applications of Koszul Pairs* (coauthored with Dragoș Ștefan), SIGMA 12 (2016), 092, 24 pages (arXiv [here](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1504.03548)).

I've also published a philosophy of science article:

* *The Technically Manifolded (Classical and Quantum) Space Ontology*, Society and Politics, Vol. 11, No. 1, 2017, pp. 91-93 (available [here](https://socpol.uvvg.ro/docs/2017-1/7_Manea.pdf)).

Furthermore, I collaborated with Dr. Marius Cosmeanu, as a scientific advisor
for his Romanian translation of *Linked: The New Science of Networks*, by
Albert László Barabási (published by Brumar in 2017).
